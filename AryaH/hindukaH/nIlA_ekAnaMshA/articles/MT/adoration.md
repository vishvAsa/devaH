There is no tAntrika material in the harivaMsha. ekAnaMshA is one of the names under which the transfunctional goddess is praised in the famous devI-stotra uttered by pradyumna found in the harivaMsha.

> I wondered whether this/similar form has a Tantrika material associated with it in another text like Prapanchasarasara(by Girvananda Saraswati) or Sharada Tilaka and the like.

Not those two popular ones, but I have seen a paddhati which worships her on aShTami following mantra-s that explicitly identify her with durgA but uses a shrIkula mUlamantra. It begins by citing a verse attributed to the bhaviShyat-purANa:

ekAnaMshAM tathA devIM tadA saMpUjayen narAH |
sarva-kAma-samR^iddhasya yaj~nasya labhate phalaM ||

tadA aShTamyAm ekAnaMshAM durgAm pUjayet |
