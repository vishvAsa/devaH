I've been puzzled by the evolution of pi~n~nai in the tamiL tradition noted here. In the earlier stratum of Tam works pi~n~nai is depicted in the middle of the vAsudeva and the baladeva. This position is reserved for ekAnaMshA in the harivaMsha. 

In the later strata of Tam works, she is clearly a consort. The earlier stratum is roughly (setting aside the controversies of dating texts like paripATal) coeval with the depictions of the core sAtvata triad of vAsudeva-ekAnaMshA-saMkarShaNa throughout the Indosphere, including in the drAviDa country. The shakti of the sAtvata strand of the vaiShNava tradition can appear as both a sister-figure and a consort. Hence, I've felt that the earlier references to pi~n~nai should be taken as ekAnaMshA, with a subsequent transformation towards a consort figure. 

This transition seems to have tracked the emergence of classical pA~ncharAtrika tAntrism and the fading away of the older version of the sAtvata, which attached importance to ekAnaMshA and the sAtvata-vIra-s, like sAtyaki.+++(4)+++
