बहुरूपः स देवेशः कुमार एक एव सः ।
उभौ स्कन्दो विशाखश्च द्विरूपोऽसौ प्रजायते ॥ १ ॥

शाखो विशाखश्च स एव देवो
ख्यातो महासेन इति त्रिधासौ ।
चतुर्विधः स्कन्द-विशाख-शाखास्
तथाजवक्त्रश्च स नेजमेषः ॥२॥

षष्ठ्या शक्त्या समन्वितः पञ्चरूपोऽभिधीयते ।
षडाननो मन्त्रपतिर् वचद्भूर् इति विश्रुतः ॥३॥

मन्त्राध्यक्षः स पातु नः षड्वक्त्रः शक्ति-संयुतः ।
कुमारो देवसेनानीः सर्वापद्भ्यो विमोचयेत् ॥४॥

1. Many indeed are the forms of that leader of the Gods; as One, he is indeed Kumāra. As both Skanda and Viśākha, he is brought forth in a twofold form.
2. That very same deity is celebrated as threefold under the names Śākha, Viśākha, and Mahāsena. And he is fourfold as Skanda, Viśākha, Śākha, and that goat-faced [deity] known as Nejameṣa.
3. Conjoined with his consort, who is Ṣaṣṭhī, he is declared to be fivefold. As the six-headed Lord of Mantras, he is widely renowned by the name Vacadbhū.
4. May he, the presiding Lord of Mantras, six-faced and conjoined with his Śakti, protect us! May Kumāra, the general of the divine hosts, deliver us from all calamities!

भूमौ निखाता जगद्-अक्ष-कल्पा
तुङ्गा महाशक्तिर् अपातयद् या |
प्रह्लाद-दैत्यं भृशम् उद्धरन्तं
सा पातु नः षण्मुख-शक्तिर् उग्रा ||

1. That great spear, standing tall, made like the world-axis when planted deep into the earth, brought the Daitya Prahlāda crashing down as he struggled forcefully to wrench it out – may that fierce spear of the Six-faced One protect us!
