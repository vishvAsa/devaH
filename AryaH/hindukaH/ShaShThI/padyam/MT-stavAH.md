For devasenA pa~nchamI:
धनदा वसुर् ईशाना  
पुण्या षष्ठी यशस्विनी ।  
नन्दी भूतिस् तथा लक्ष्मीर्  
आदित्यापि च कामदा ॥१॥

सुमनाः स्कन्दपत्नी च  
तथा वाक् सिद्धिर् आकृतिः।  
रजनी विश्वरूपा च  
वर्चनी पद्मचारिणी ॥२॥

प्रकृतिः कामपत्नी च  
मन्मना सा च धावनी ।
उपलक्ष्मीर् हरिद्रा च  
समृद्धिश् च तथा जया ॥३॥

स्कन्दप्रिया सा वरदा च षष्ठी  
नाम्नाम् अनेन स्तवनेन तुष्टा ।  
आरोग्य-सौभाग्य-यशांसि देवी  
यच्छत्व् अभीष्टां परमाशिषं नः  ॥४॥

1. She is Dhanadā (Giver of Wealth), Vasu (Treasure), Īśānā (Sovereign Mistress), Puṇyā (Meritorious), Ṣaṣṭhī (Goddess of the Sixth), and Yaśasvinī (Glorious); she is Nandī (Joyful), Bhūti (Prosperity), and Lakṣmī (Fortune), and likewise she is Ādityā (Solar goddess) and Kāmadā (Granter of Desires).

2. She is Sumanā (Benevolent-Minded) and Skandapatnī (Consort of Skanda), as well as Vāk (Sacred Speech), Siddhi (Perfection), and Ākṛti (Form); she is Rajanī (Night), Viśvarūpā (Omniform), Varcanī (Radiant), and Padmacāriṇī (Abiding in the lotus). (2)

3. She is Prakṛti (Primal Nature) and Kāmapatnī (The Mistress of love), as well as Manmanā (Love) and Dhāvanī (Swift-Moving/Purifying); she is Upalakṣmī (Attendant Fortune), Haridrā (Golden-Hued), Samṛddhi (Abundance), and likewise Jayā (Victory).

4. Pleased by this hymn of her [twenty-eight] names, may that boon-granting Goddess Ṣaṣṭhī, the beloved consort of Skanda, bestow health, good fortune, and glory (yaśāṃsi), and graciously grant her wished-for, supreme blessing upon us!
