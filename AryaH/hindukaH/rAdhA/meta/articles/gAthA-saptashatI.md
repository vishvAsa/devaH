> Always used to think rAdhA legend is from brahma-vaivarta purANa and jayadeva's gIta govinda

Oldest mention of Radha is around 2000 yrs old.

मुहमारुएण तं कण्ह गोरअं राहिआइ अवणेंतो ।  
एआण वल्लवीणं अण्णाण वि गोरअं हरसि ॥  
Krishna, as you blow the cow-dust off Radhika's face with the breath of your mouth, you take away, from these other cowherd women, their pride as well.

This is from the Gatha Saptashati, a MH Prakrit poem from the Satavahana court dated to the first century.


> It's uncertain whether it genuinely belongs to the Sātavāhana period.
Some verses in the collection date to time of Vākpatirāja. Gāhāsattasaī is an anthology that was likely compiled in the Deccan some time during the first millennium of the Common Era, so Dravidian influence in the Gāhāsattasaī is not unexpected, though the name "Rāhiā" (and possibly the figure herself) is Indo-Aryan in origin. - saatvata
