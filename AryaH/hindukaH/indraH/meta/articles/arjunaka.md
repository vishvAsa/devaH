It is known to us that a sectarian order to Arjuna (Indra) persisted into Pāṇini's time (500s/400s BC) as part of a cultic-moiety of sorts where the former was in the process of being subdued and assimilated by these early Bhāgavata-Vaiṣṇava-s.

See - The Beginnings of Vaiṣṇavism (1976) by R.N. Dandekar
