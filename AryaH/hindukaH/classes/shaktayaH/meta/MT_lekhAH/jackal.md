This distinctive iconography matches the dhyAna provided in the Kashmirian (Anandeshvara) paddhati for th devI-yAga of shivA bhagavatI; however, there she is 8 armed:

devIM sR^igAla-vadanAm aruNAM trinetrAm ekAnanAM vasu-bhujAM shasi-shUla-hastAm |  
pAshA~NkushAM dhR^ita-kapAlAM sa-ki~NkiNIkAM khaTvA~Nga-pustakadharAM shivagAM namAmi ||  
tatpuruShAyai vidmahe shivArUpAyai dhImahi | tan no devI prachodayAt || x 3  
OM hrIM shrIM shivA-bhagavatyai namaH svAhA || x 108  

Otherwise, it is customary to describe guhyakAlI as having the form of a jackal:
*shivA-rUpadhare* devi guhyakAli namo .astu te ||
ulkAmukhi lalaj-jihve ghorarAve *shR^igAlini* |
shmashAna-vAshini prete shava-mAMsa-priye .anaghe ||
araNyachAriNy anaghe *shive jambuka-rUpiNi* |
