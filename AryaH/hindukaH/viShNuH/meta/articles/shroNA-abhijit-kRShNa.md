Abhijit is the only "masculine nakṣatra."

The other 27 nakṣatras are personified as daughters of Dakṣa and wives of the moon whereas Abhijit being an intercalary/supplemental nakṣatra is personified as their brother.

The moon does not "spend the night" with Abhijit in the traditional cyclical sense since he is the "brother-in-law" of the moon.

Abhijit lies between Uttarāṣāḍha and Śravaṇa/Śroṇā, as mentioned in the Vedas.

"Abhijín nā́ma nákṣatram upáriṣṭād aṣāḍhā́nām adhástāc Chroṇā́yāḥ (vl. avástāc Chroṇā́yai)"

Uttarāṣāḍha is governed by the Viśvedevas while Śravaṇa/Śroṇā is governed by Viṣṇu (in his Vāmana/Trivikrama aspect).

Kṛṣṇa was born under Abhijit (Abhijin nāma nakṣatraṃ, Jayantī nāma śarvarī. muhūrto Vijayo nāma, yatra jāto Janārddanaḥ).

In the Uddhava-Gītā he states that he is Abhijit among Nakṣatras (māsānāṃ Mārgaśīrṣo 'haṃ nakṣatrāṇāṃ tathābhijit) while in the Bhagavad-Gītā he says he is śaśin/the moon among Nakṣatras (Marīcir Marutām asmi, nakṣatrāṇām ahaṃ Śaśī).
