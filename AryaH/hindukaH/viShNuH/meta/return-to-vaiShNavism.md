## Coantiquity of vaiShNavism

>  their sampradayas started as heterodox Vedic offshoots that would be considered larp by Ṛgvedic Ṛṣis

I disagree with this - vaiShNava-s, forming the later ascendent Adhvaryava tradition, were a sect well known to pioneering Rgvedic RShi-s (ie the hautra tradition which dominated the frontline), and soon followed and took over from them during the consolidation phase. Please see -  [TW](https://manasataramgini.wordpress.com/2020/01/02/the-roots-of-vai%e1%b9%a3%e1%b9%87avam-a-view-from-the-numerology-of-vedic-texts/), [TW](https://mravinderreddy.substack.com/p/introduction-to-visnu), [TW](https://x.com/blog_supplement/status/2053719048597188864)

To quote MT - 


> 5\. This group IMO was already diversified into a dialect & religious substructure.  
- The front end of the wave was led by IA speakers with a hotraka "indra-para" religious core. At their rear were the ancestors of the Kalasha & probably Brokpa (drokpa). There were also probably some minor western Corded Ware descendants in their midst given the Bangani evidence. They already arrived with the much of the RV composed (contra mainstream BS). They had left behind on the steppe on other IA & Ir groups.
- On the steppe, just behind the first wave was an IA group with a distinct religious approach -- Adhvaryava & viShNu-focal with some rudra-focal elements scattered in their midst. 
- A more rudra-focal group lay on the steppes in the borderland with the Ir. 
- The initial IA empire/confederation straddled the steppe & north India (like kuShANa of historic times). During this period of straddling, the second IA group with the Adhvaryava tradition entered India & became dominant in a synthesis with the old hautra tradition resulting in the Indian variant of the Vedic religion.
- This period also saw the entry of IA groups with affinities to Ir that brought the core AV with them. 
- More of such Ir-affiliated groups either IA-speaking but close to Ir folks or Ir-speaking invaded in the subsequent centuries resulting in the events of the extant mahAbhArata -- the pANDu conquest.

## Competition and dominance
This is not to deny that there was competition between the aindra-para hautra, prAjapatya, vaiShNava Adhvaryava and the para-vedic raudra sects; or that this involve usurping each others' myths. All that was part of a natural, healthy evolutionary race which the aindra hautra tradition lost long ago - basically subsumed under the now victorious and ubiquitous Adhvaryava yajur-vaidikas.

So, while the Rgvedic aindra dominance is the natural starting point of the honest student of the veida-s, the mature position involves consideration of the non-Rgvedic and para-vedic cults of our sagely ancestors. Hence the return to the bearer of the wheel.

## Modern vaiShNava perspective
What this means for us (those wise enough, as divine Sallustius might say, to see into the heart of sacred myths), in hindsight, is that the Gods are many and in great harmony, with supremacy claims being cultic highs.

And indeed, the first two Ahuti-s every fortnight during sthAlIpAka go to prajApati and indra, dear brother & friend of our great God. This being the case, who can gainsay our devotion to the many Gods of the pantheon just because we're partisans of viShNu?
