In the Orphic tradition, there arose a goddess known as Physis, who in many ways resembles the Hindu deifications of the sAMkhya prakR^iti. The Orphic hymn accompanying the offering of spices to Physis is a string of 81 epithets. In general terms, this genre of hymns in the Orphic corpus closely parallels the nAmAvalI-s and nAmasaMgIti-s in the Indo-Iranian tradition. Several of the general epithets have parallels in the divine epithets used in Hindu nAmAvalI-s. However, one wonders if the choice of the number 81 was deliberate and had to do with it being $3^4=9^2$. It reminds one of the ekAshIti-pada mantra in shaiva tradition. Below is Malamis' translation, followed by the transliteration of the Greek original in case you want to check it:

    You are all queen: you alone bring all this to pass.
    But, goddess, I pray you, in prosperous seasons
    bring peace, health and increase of all.
